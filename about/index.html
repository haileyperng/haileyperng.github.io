About

Filmmaker, photographer and live-broadcast director from Southern California.

Portrait

I'm Hailey Perng. I learned to tell stories behind the camera at the Orange County School of the Arts, where I directed live broadcasts, filmed and edited campaigns, and helped make an award-winning yearbook for 2,400+ students.

Now I work where production meets marketing: studying what makes content travel as a Marketing Analyst, and shooting films and senior portraits through Perng Production. Along the way I've taught younger students how to run a camera and conduct an interview. I also speak Mandarin natively.
